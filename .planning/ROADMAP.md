# ROADMAP: new-house-management

**Created:** 2026-09-19

## Overview
A short roadmap split into phased milestones to reach a usable MVP quickly.

## Phase 1 — MVP: Inventory, Budget, Timeline (2–4 weeks)
- Goal: Allow users to capture inventory, add costs, and create a move timeline.
- Deliverables:
  - Inventory capture UI/CLI (INV-01..INV-03)
  - Budget summary (BUD-01..BUD-03)
  - Timeline editor and checklist (TIM-01..TIM-03)
- Acceptance criteria:
  - Users can add 10 items, record costs, and export inventory as CSV.
  - Timeline milestones display upcoming due dates.
- Estimated duration: 2 weeks

## Phase 2 — Vendor tracking & purchase flow (2 weeks)
- Goal: Track vendors and link them to items and costs.
- Deliverables:
  - Vendor contact records (VEND-01..VEND-02)
  - Simple purchase order note capture
- Acceptance criteria:
  - Add and search vendors; link vendor to a budget item.
- Estimated duration: 2 weeks

## Phase 3 — Polish, export, and small automations (2 weeks)
- Goal: Improve UX, add exports, and automations (reminders, summaries).
- Deliverables:
  - Reminder export, improved CSV/print views
  - Basic automation: recurring reminders for payments
- Acceptance criteria:
  - Reminders can be exported and scheduled.
- Estimated duration: 2 weeks
